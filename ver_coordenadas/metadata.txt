about=Plugin for viewing and copying coordinates and measurements from Polygon, MultiPolygon, LineString and MultiLineString features. Projected CRS coordinates can be displayed in Gauss-Krüger order (northing, easting) or traditional order (easting, northing). Geographic CRS coordinates are available in DMS, DMM and decimal degrees, with ellipsoidal measurements through QgsDistanceArea and contextual warnings. Net polygon areas, hole areas and multipart totals are also reported. Curved geometries, invalid CRS definitions and projected CRS with unknown units are rejected explicitly.

    Rechaza geometrías curvas no soportadas de forma explícita.
    Valida CRS inválidos y unidades proyectadas desconocidas antes de calcular medidas métricas.
